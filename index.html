Meet Kiarra

The girl behind Bare Beauty by K

Welcome to my studio! I'm completely beauty-obsessed and dedicated to giving you that effortless, flawless look.

When I'm not in the studio perfecting brows and lashes, I'm usually navigating the beautiful chaos of being a boy mum, fueling up on vanilla lattes, or curled up at home with a spicy book.

I'm a proud Aries, a professional overthinker, and a homebody at heart (though I won't say no to a sneaky girls' night!). I can't wait to welcome you in.
